# Wok Star

Wok Star — an original Indonesian street-food cooking game for make.fun

Run a night-market warung stall: assemble dishes from 12 ingredients, cook them on the stove, and serve orders before their tickets expire. A 3-minute shift, combo bonuses, and tips for fast serves.

## Play

Serve it locally with `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.

## Gameplay

- **Menu:** Nasi Goreng, Mie Ayam, Sate Ayam (cooked on the stove), and Es Teh Manis (served straight).
- **Assemble:** click ingredients on the counter (or keys 1–9, 0, -, =) to build the dish on the plate. A wrong ingredient bounces back.
- **Cook:** press Space or click the stove to cook a finished plate — 4 seconds on a burner, then serve before it burns.
- **Serve:** press Enter or click the serving window to hand the dish to the matching order. Fast serves earn up to +50% tip, and consecutive serves build a combo.
- **Shift:** 3 minutes, 3 expired orders and the warung closes. P pauses, R restarts, M mutes.
